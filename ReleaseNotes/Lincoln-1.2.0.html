Lincoln 1.2.0

Lincoln 1.2.0 lets you rename tunnels right in the sidebar and keeps the main window's contents inside the window at any size.

  • Rename from the sidebar: Right-click a tunnel and choose Rename, or choose Rename Tunnel from the actions menu in the detail pane. Press Return or click away to save, or Escape to cancel. Unsaved edits in the editor are kept.
  • Fix: The forwards table, the tunnel header and the sidebar rows no longer spill past the edge of the window when it's narrow. Long names are shortened with an ellipsis, and the main window can't get narrower than its contents.
