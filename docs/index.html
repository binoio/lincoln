Lincoln

Establish, maintain and reconfigure SSH tunnels through your gateway — SOCKS proxies and port forwards, from the Dock or the menu bar.

Download for macOS

Just Tunnels

Define a tunnel once, then connect and disconnect it with one click from the menu bar.

Shared with Terminal

Tunnels use the Control Path from your ssh config, so ssh re-uses them with no repeated prompts.

Reads Your ssh config

Imports host aliases and existing forwards from ~/.ssh/config and runs ssh with your normal configuration. It never writes to it.

Durable & Idempotent

Tunnels and their desired state live in Application Support; masters outlive the app. Reinstall a year later and pick up exactly where you left off.
